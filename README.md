BUSINESS & SERVICES:
Royal Ride provides luxury cars, stretch limousines, and premium transportation for special occasions, corporate events, and airport transfers.
The main services to include are:
- Stretch & Party Limousines
- Premium Luxury Sedans & SUVs
- Wedding Transportation
- Corporate & Executive Travel
- Birthdays & Special Celebrations
- Airport Transfers

COLOR PALETTE & DESIGN STYLE:
Style: Ultra-luxury, elegant, premium, and clean. It must look like a multi-million dollar business, not a cheap template.
Colors: 
- Deep Black (#0B0B0D) and Charcoal (#17171A) as main backgrounds.
- Warm Champagne Gold (#C8A96B) for buttons, active states, and elegant borders.
- Soft Ivory (#F5F2EA) and pure White (#FFFFFF) for text.
Typography: Elegant serif font for major headings (e.g., Cormorant Garamond style) and a super clean modern sans-serif font for body text.

UX/UI REQUIREMENTS:
- Extremely responsive and flawless on mobile devices.

- Add very
